Envoy Finance:   Privacy Policy

Envoy Finance Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Envoy Finance is an internal software tool owned and operated by Envoy Development LLC ("Envoy," "we," "us"). It is used only by authorized Envoy staff to manage Envoy's own accounting records. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

What information the app accesses

With Envoy's authorization, Envoy Finance connects to Envoy's own QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's API. It may read accounting records such as company information, customers, invoices, and payments.

How the information is used

The information is used only for Envoy's internal finance work, such as reconciling payments against invoices and preparing internal reports.

How the information is stored and protected

  • Connection credentials and access tokens are stored in the secure macOS Keychain on Envoy-controlled computers.
  • Any data the app saves is kept on Envoy-controlled computers or systems.
  • Access is limited to authorized Envoy staff.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks data obtained through Envoy Finance with third parties, except where required by law.

Disconnecting

Envoy can revoke the app's access at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings → Apps → Envoy Finance → Disconnect). After that, the app can no longer access QuickBooks data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: accounting@envoydevelopment.com
