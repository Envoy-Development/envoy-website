Website Privacy Notice

Effective November 20, 2026

This notice explains what information Envoy Development, LLC ("Envoy", "we") collects through this website, and how we use it.

1. What this notice covers

This notice covers the Envoy public website. It does not cover the Envoy platform or the services we provide to customers. Customer data in the platform is handled under each customer's agreement with Envoy and its data processing terms.

2. Information we collect

Information you send us. When you use the contact form or email us, we receive what you choose to send. This can include your name, email address, company and site, phone number, industry, the reason for your inquiry and your message. Our hosting provider processes the contact form and delivers it to our sales inbox. Demo bookings are made through Microsoft Bookings, which collects the name, email address and details you enter when you book.

Technical information. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs when a page is requested. These logs can include IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date and time.

Assessment answers. If you take the assessment, your answers are saved in your own browser so the page can show your result. They are not sent to Envoy. You can remove them by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies. It does not use advertising or social media tracking tools.

We count page views with our hosting provider's analytics. It works from server logs. It does not use cookies and does not identify you. If this changes, we will update this notice first.

4. How we use information

We use the information you send us to:

  1. Reply to your inquiry or demo request.
  2. Provide support if you are a customer.
  3. Keep a record of our business contacts.
  4. Keep the website secure and working.

We do not use website inquiries to train AI models.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with:

  1. Service providers that host this website and handle our email and business records, under terms that require them to protect it.
  2. Authorities, when the law requires it.
  3. A successor company, if Envoy is part of a merger or sale.

6. How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and contact records for as long as we need them for the purposes above, and then delete them in line with our records retention policy.

7. Security

We protect personal information with technical and organizational controls that fit its sensitivity. These include encryption in transit, access limited to the people who need it, and multi-factor authentication on our business systems. No website can promise complete security.

8. Your choices

You can ask us to give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Send your request to sales@envoydevelopment.com. We confirm who you are before we act on a request. We reply within one month.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you other rights. We honor the rights that apply to you.

9. Children

This website is for business users. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this notice

When we change this notice, we post the new version on this page and change the effective date.
