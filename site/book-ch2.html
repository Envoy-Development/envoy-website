Where is your plant?

Spread 1

Maturity is how decisions get made

Maturity is not how new your equipment is or how much data you store. It is how your plant makes its decisions, shift after shift. Five levels describe it.

  1. Unstable. Problems find you. Every shift starts with something that broke overnight.
  2. Stable. You know what is wrong, and you are fixing it in order.
  3. Repeatable. Every crew runs the process the same way, to the same targets.
  4. Optimized. You know the best way to run, not only a consistent way.
  5. Adaptive. What you learn goes back into the standard, so the next shift starts smarter.

Each level has a trap. It feels like progress, but it holds you in place.

Spread 2

Why AI sold at the wrong stage fails

Each level needs a different kind of help. Unstable and Stable plants need eyes: something that sees every change overnight and puts a short list in front of the team. Repeatable and Optimized plants need targets: where to run, and proof that it costs the least. Adaptive plants need memory: every past case, ready when the same symptom comes back.

Most failed AI projects skip a level. An optimizer sold into an unstable process has nothing steady to optimize. A cost model built on targets nobody runs to will never pay out. The tool was right. The stage was wrong.

Your plant is rarely at one level. You can be Repeatable on where everything is set and Unstable on what to fix first. That is normal, and it tells you where to start. Start with the decision at the lowest level, because it holds the others back.

Chapter 12 lets you score your own plant in ten minutes. The next chapter is about what AI can and cannot do at any level.

In practice. Envoy builds its services to match the levels: daily process reporting for levels 1 and 2, Smart Centerlines for levels 3 and 4, and Envoy Intelligence for level 5.
