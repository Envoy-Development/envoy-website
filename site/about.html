Our Story

Deep roots in the mill.
A broader view of what's possible.

Founded in 2003, Envoy began by helping pulp and paper mills evaluate advanced control solutions. That work grew into an ongoing engineering partnership, with daily reviews of process data and deeper investigations into the issues affecting each operation.

Today, our operational intelligence platform combines physics-based analytics, artificial intelligence, and the guidance of experienced process engineers. We are building on our pulp-and-paper foundation as we extend our focus to food and specialty chemicals.

What We Believe

How we show up for your team.

Good engineering support is practical, collaborative, and grounded in the realities of your operation.

Insight should lead to action.

We focus on findings your team can use: what changed, why it matters, and where to investigate next.

Experience gives data context.

Physics-based analytics and AI become more useful when guided by engineers who understand the process, its operating conditions, and the questions worth asking.

Long-term value is earned.

We build relationships through useful work, responsive support, and a continuing understanding of your plant. Sharing knowledge helps your team grow stronger over time.

Be the team you call when the answer isn't obvious.

Developing problems rarely come with a complete explanation. We work with your people to investigate causes and make sense of the evidence.
