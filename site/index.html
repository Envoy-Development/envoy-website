Built for Established Plants

Work with the systems you already have.

A plant can contain decades of equipment, different control systems, and uneven access to process history. Envoy is designed for that reality.

Our vendor-agnostic approach works across mixed equipment and data environments. We connect to available sources, use an existing historian where one is in place, and can provide historian capability where it is needed.

Start with a priority process area. We work with your team to understand the data, equipment and operating questions that matter there.

A practical fit for your operation

  • A mix of equipment generations and control-system vendors.
  • An existing historian, gaps in coverage, or a need to establish process history.
  • Limited engineering bandwidth and recurring problems that need deeper investigation.
  • New hires and experienced employees who need to share process knowledge.

An Extension of Your Team

Experienced engineers who stay involved.

The value of a finding depends on understanding the process behind it. Envoy’s engineers help interpret what changed, why it matters, and what your team should investigate next.

That support starts with clear daily priorities and extends to difficult troubleshooting, operating-target development and knowledge transfer. When specialist experience is hard to hire or retain locally, your team has people to call on.

A focused morning report. A deeper investigation when you need it. Engineering support as your plant changes.
