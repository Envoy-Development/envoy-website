Control Loop Health Report

Is every loop doing its job?

We collect one-second data on every control loop in the area and run it through 25 checks. Then we sort each loop by controller type and by what it is meant to do, so a level loop is not graded like a flow loop.

  • A short list to act on. Loops in manual, at a limit or out of service are reported by state. Only loops that can control are graded.
  • A likely cause for each one. Tuning, a sticking valve, or a disturbance from upstream.
  • Valves that work too hard. Loops that hold target but wear the valve.
  • Loops that swing together. A shared cycle often points to one source.
  • Change since last month. What is new, what is fixed, and what is still open.

Monthly Maintenance Report

Find the slow problems before they cost production.

A sensor quits. A reading flatlines. A value shifts or drifts. None of these trips an alarm. Each month we test every tag in the area against its own history and list what to check.

  • Sensors that need attention. Shifted, drifting, flatlined, swinging, or no longer reporting.
  • Valves that are stuck or at a limit. Outputs that have not moved in days.
  • Equipment compared with its own history. A valve more open for the same flow. A motor drawing more load for the same duty.
  • Repeat offenders. Tags that keep returning to the morning report.
  • After an outage. The same review compares before and after the restart, including setpoints that changed.
