The Shared Data Foundation

A clearer view of the plant you already have.

Established plants rarely have one generation of equipment or one control-system vendor. Process history may be complete in one area and missing in another.

Envoy takes a vendor-agnostic approach. Connect available process-data sources and use the historian you already have. Where coverage is missing, Envoy can provide historian capability to collect and retain time-stamped process data, giving your team an operating history to review and analyze.

We work with your team to confirm the interfaces, access and data quality needed for the first application. The starting point is your operating priority and the systems available to support it.
