The Intelligent Factory:   Assessment

How intelligent is your operation today?

A structured way to gauge your operation's intelligence maturity, and see which moves would matter most next.

Talk to an Engineer ← Intelligent Factory: Start Here

How It Works

Built on the five-level maturity model.

The assessment places your operation on the curve from Reactive to Adaptive, evaluated across the seven disciplines, not just the technology inventory. Here's what you'll walk away with.

Where You Are

Your operation's current level: Reactive, Connected, Contextualized, Intelligent, or Adaptive, grounded in how decisions actually get made today.

Where the Gaps Are

Which disciplines are holding you back (often it's operational context or governance, not analytics), and why that matters more than the next tool purchase.

What to Do Next

The two or three moves that would matter most from your current position, because the right next step at Level 2 is very different from the right next step at Level 4.
