The Intelligent Factory:  The Book

Leading the Intelligent Factory

Six decisions that run every plant, and how AI makes each one better. A short, practical book for plant, operations and production managers in process industries.

Start readingScore your plant

Draft for review

The promise

You do not need a new factory or a data science team.

You are measured on reliability, quality and cost. AI does not move those on its own. It makes the decisions behind them better, in the right order for where your plant is today: reliability first, then quality, then cost.

Every chapter follows the same five beats, so you can skim any chapter and know where to look: the pressure, what goes wrong today, what AI changes, a real example, and questions for Monday morning.

Read it in under an hour. Then score your plant, and ask us to walk through the result with you.
