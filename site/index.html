Built for Established Plants

Work with the systems you already have.

A plant can contain decades of equipment, different control systems, and uneven access to process history. Envoy is designed for that reality.

Our vendor-agnostic approach works across mixed equipment and data environments. We connect to available sources, use an existing historian where one is in place, and can provide historian capability where it is needed.

Start with a priority process area. We work with your team to understand the data, equipment and operating questions that matter there.
