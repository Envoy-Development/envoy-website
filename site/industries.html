An Engineering Foundation

The platform travels.
The process context comes first.

Envoy grew from the practical work of supporting pulp and paper mills: reviewing operating data, finding meaningful changes, and helping engineers investigate difficult problems. That experience shapes how we build our software and how we deliver our service.

As we enter food and specialty chemicals, we start with the same questions. What decision does the team need to make? Which operating conditions matter? What data is available? How will we know whether the work is useful?

Each application starts with a defined process, an agreed objective, and the right engineering context.

Start with a real operating question.

  • What changed, and does it need attention?
  • Which conditions support consistent performance?
  • Where are losses or recurring disruptions coming from?
  • What should the team investigate next?
